About

I give myself one evening per site, and the constraint does the design work.

I'm Thammarat, a front-end developer in progress. This repository is a daily habit made visible: a new page every day, each one small enough to finish before midnight and honest enough to publish as it is.

There is no dashboard of fake testimonials here, no glassy cards. Just type, rules, and whatever the page actually needs. If a build is rough, it stays rough — the point is the streak, not the polish.
